# Data availability

No TCGA data, patient identifiers, pathology images, molecular matrices, embeddings, predictions, checkpoints, or feature caches are distributed. Researchers must obtain authorized data from their source and comply with its access and use terms.

The synthetic CSV is generated locally by examples/synthetic_example/generate.py. It is for import, split, preprocessing, training, and evaluation smoke checks only; it is not representative clinical data and cannot support a biomedical claim.
